## AI/ML Intern — ICAR-NBSS&LUP, Nagpur

Vedant completed an offline AI/ML internship at ICAR-NBSS&LUP, Nagpur.
Internship duration: January 2026 – June 2026.
His internship focused on applying AI/ML, GIS, remote sensing, and geospatial analysis to real-world environmental problems.

## Internship Project

Vedant worked on an Intelligent Decision Support System (DSS) for Soil Erosion and Land Degradation.
A major focus of the project was developing a Digital Soil Loss Information System for the Vidarbha region.
The system was designed to support soil erosion assessment, analysis, visualization, and decision-making.
